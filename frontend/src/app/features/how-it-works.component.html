ONE THOUGHTFUL SPACE

A place for the whole picture.

Accounts & transactions

See recorded balances and net worth. Search, filter, edit, and export transactions; transfers update both account records together.

Budgets that make sense

Plan an overall monthly limit or focus on expense categories. Clear progress and notifications show when spending gets close to the plan.

Bills & subscriptions

Keep due dates and recurring costs in view. Record payments you already made, with repeat-request protection for each due date.

Goals worth saving for

Track money earmarked for an emergency fund, a memorable trip, or your next chapter. Contributions measure progress independently of bank balances.

Reports with perspective

Explore monthly income, expenses, cashflow, and spending categories. Accessible chart tables and CSV exports make the details easy to review.

Care built into the details

Protected sessions, optional MFA, account recovery, and a record of important actions help you keep control of your financial information.
